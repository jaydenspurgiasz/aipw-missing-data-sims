{\LARGE\bfseries AIPW M-estimation and CFA variance reference}\par
\smallskip
\textbf{Purpose.} Fast reference for the estimator, analytic AIPW Jacobian, sandwich variance, CFA Jacobian, simulation metrics, and current CFA limitations.

\section*{1. Target and reported parameter blocks}
\[
\Sigma=E[XX^T]-\mu\mu^T,\qquad
\gamma=(\mu,\nu),\quad \mu=E[X],\quad \nu=\vech(E[XX^T]).
\]

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}l c Y Y@{}}
\toprule
Block & Count & Estimated quantity & Calculation \\
\midrule
$\beta$ & 12 & Outcome-model intercept and $Z$ slope for each of 6 outcomes & Observed-case linear score \\
$\alpha$ & 2 & Shared logistic response-model coefficients & Binomial-logit response score \\
$\gamma=(\mu,\nu)$ & 27 & 6 means and 21 raw second moments & AIPW moment equations \\
$(\Lambda,\Phi,\Omega)$ & 13 & 6 loadings, 1 factor correlation, 6 uniquenesses & Two-factor CFA fitted to $\Sigma$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

Default report: $(\beta,\alpha,\gamma,\Lambda,\Phi,\Omega)$, 54 coordinates. $\psi$ is the estimating-function vector, not another parameter block. The code additionally estimates residual means $\rho$ and residual covariance $\sigma$ internally. They are needed for correct uncertainty propagation but are not primary reported targets.

\section*{2. Estimating equations}
Let $m_{ij}=z_i^T\beta_j$, $\pi_i=\operatorname{expit}(z_i^T\alpha)$, $q_{ijk}=m_{ij}m_{ik}+\sigma_{jk}$, and $w_{ijk}=R_{ij}R_{ik}/\pi_i^e$, where $e=1$ if $j=k$ and $e=2$ otherwise.

\begin{align*}
\psi_{\beta_j,i} &= R_{ij}z_i(X_{ij}-z_i^T\beta_j),\\
\psi_{\alpha,i} &= z_i\left(\sum_jR_{ij}-p\pi_i\right),\\
\psi_{\mu_j,i} &= m_{ij}+\frac{R_{ij}}{\pi_i}(X_{ij}-m_{ij})-\mu_j,\\
\psi_{\nu_{jk},i} &= q_{ijk}+w_{ijk}(X_{ij}X_{ik}-q_{ijk})-\nu_{jk}.
\end{align*}

The fitted values solve $n^{-1}\sum_i\psi_i(\hat\theta)=0$. Thus $\hat\gamma$ is exactly the AIPW estimate of the first and raw second moments.

\section*{3. Analytic AIPW Jacobian}
\[
A=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n\frac{\partial\psi_i}{\partial\theta^T}.
\]

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}l Y Y@{}}
\toprule
Derivative & Formula or computation & Interpretation \\
\midrule
$\partial\psi_{\mu_j}/\partial\mu_j$ & $-1$ & Moment parameter is directly targeted. \\
$\partial\psi_{\mu_j}/\partial\beta_j$ & $(1-R_{ij}/\pi_i)z_i$ & Outcome-model uncertainty enters $\mu_j$. \\
$\partial\psi_{\mu_j}/\partial\alpha$ & $-[R_{ij}/\pi_i](X_{ij}-m_{ij})(1-\pi_i)z_i$ & Propensity uncertainty enters $\mu_j$. \\
$\partial\psi_{\nu_{jk}}/\partial\nu_{jk}$ & $-1$ & Raw second moment is directly targeted. \\
$\partial\psi_{\nu_{jk}}/\partial\beta$ & Differentiate $q_{ijk}$; diagonal uses $2m_{ij}z_i$, off diagonal uses $m_{ik}z_i$ or $m_{ij}z_i$ & Outcome-model uncertainty enters $\nu_{jk}$. \\
$\partial\psi_{\nu_{jk}}/\partial\alpha$ & $-e\,w_{ijk}(X_{ij}X_{ik}-q_{ijk})(1-\pi_i)z_i$ & Propensity uncertainty enters $\nu_{jk}$. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

The meaningful block pattern is
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
 & \beta & \alpha & \gamma & (\Lambda,\Phi,\Omega)\\ \hline
\psi_\beta & A_{\beta\beta} & 0 & 0 & 0\\
\psi_\alpha & 0 & A_{\alpha\alpha} & 0 & 0\\
\psi_\gamma & A_{\gamma\beta} & A_{\gamma\alpha} & -I & 0\\
\psi_{\mathrm{CFA}} & 0 & 0 & A_{\mathrm{CFA},\gamma} & A_{\mathrm{CFA},\mathrm{CFA}}
\end{array}.
\]

\section*{4. Sandwich variance and covariance map}
\[
B=\frac{1}{n}\sum_i\psi_i\psi_i^T,\qquad
\widehat V_\theta=A^{-1}BA^{-T}/n.
\]

The diagonal of $\widehat V_\theta$ contains marginal variances; its square roots are standard errors. For the covariance map, $\Sigma_{jk}=\nu_{jk}-\mu_j\mu_k$, so
\[
\frac{\partial\Sigma_{jk}}{\partial\nu_{jk}}=1,\qquad
\frac{\partial\Sigma_{jk}}{\partial\mu_\ell}
=-\mathbf1(\ell=j)\mu_k-\mathbf1(\ell=k)\mu_j.
\]
If $D=\partial\vech(\Sigma)/\partial\gamma^T$, then
\[
\widehat V_{\vech(\Sigma)}=D\widehat V_\gamma D^T.
\]

\section*{5. CFA fit and the computational CFA Jacobian}
The CFA map is $h:\vech(S)\mapsto(\hat\Lambda,\hat\Phi,\hat\Omega)$, with $S=\hat\Sigma_{\mathrm{AIPW}}$. The implementation uses the Gaussian covariance objective
\[
\frac{n}{2}\left\{\log|\Sigma_{\mathrm{CFA}}|+\operatorname{tr}(S\Sigma_{\mathrm{CFA}}^{-1})\right\}
\]
and L-BFGS-B through \texttt{factor\_analyzer}.

\textbf{How $G=\partial h/\partial\vech(S)^T$ is computed.}
There are $6(6+1)/2=21$ unique entries of $S$. For coordinate $\ell$ corresponding to $(j,k)$:
\begin{enumerate}[nosep,leftmargin=18pt]
\item Set $h_\ell=c\sqrt{S_{jj}S_{kk}}$; $c$ is a relative step size.
\item Form $S^+=S+h_\ell H_\ell$ and $S^-=S-h_\ell H_\ell$. If $j\ne k$, $H_\ell$ changes \emph{both} $S_{jk}$ and $S_{kj}$ by one. No factor of one-half is used.
\item Require both perturbed matrices to be positive definite.
\item Refit CFA to $S^+$ and $S^-$. Align each loading block's sign to the base fit; adjust the factor correlation for any sign flip.
\item Store the central difference column:
\[
G_{\cdot\ell}=\frac{h(S^+)-h(S^-)}{2h_\ell}.
\]
\end{enumerate}

Then
\[
\widehat V_{\mathrm{CFA}}=G\widehat V_{\vech(S)}G^T.
\]
No additional division by $n$ is allowed: the incoming sandwich covariance already includes $1/n$.

\section*{6. Simulation metrics and execution}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}l Y Y@{}}
\toprule
Metric & Calculation & Use \\
\midrule
Empirical variance & Sample covariance of estimates over successful replications & Monte Carlo benchmark \\
Predicted variance & Mean of per-replication sandwich covariance matrices & M-estimation prediction \\
Variance ratio & Predicted marginal variance / empirical marginal variance & Target: near 1 \\
Contribution & Parameter marginal variance divided by trace of the reported 54-coordinate covariance & Share of displayed marginal uncertainty \\
Total variance & $\operatorname{tr}(V)=\sum_rV_{rr}$ & Compact accounting summary only \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

The 54-coordinate trace includes both $\gamma$ and its derived CFA parameters. It is not an independent-information total; for inference use an individual variance or the relevant covariance block.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Real data / one sample:} 3 finite-difference step sizes and 3 random directional checks.
\item \textbf{Simulation pilot:} same full validation when \texttt{RUN\_DERIVATIVE\_VALIDATION=True}.
\item \textbf{Simulation production:} one prevalidated $10^{-5}$ step and no repeated directional checks; joblib process parallelism with a progress bar. This shortcut does not alter the one-sample M-estimation calculation.
\end{itemize}

\section*{7. Current CFA limitations and checks}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}l Y Y@{}}
\toprule
Issue & Why it matters & Current handling \\
\midrule
PSD projection & Eigenvalue clipping is nonsmooth; differentiating through it is invalid. & Use raw positive-definite AIPW covariance and reject non-PD perturbations. \\
Numerical derivative & $G$ may depend on step size or optimizer noise. & Pilot compares three step sizes and random directional derivatives. \\
Optimizer status & A finite output is not a complete convergence diagnostic. & Current wrapper checks finite/structural validity; expose optimizer diagnostics in future work. \\
Identification & Equivalent sign orientations can jump across CFA refits. & Canonicalize base solution and sign-align perturbations. \\
Boundary solutions & Heywood cases and factor correlation near $\pm1$ weaken local normal theory. & Reject nonpositive uniquenesses, near-zero loading blocks, and $|\phi|\ge0.999$. \\
Auxiliary $\rho,\sigma$ & Omitting their estimation uncertainty understates variance. & Include internally in sandwich; hide from default report. \\
Simulation failures & Dropping failures can bias calibration. & Record failure count and reasons before interpreting results. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\section*{8. Quick review checklist}
\begin{itemize}
\item Verify max absolute mean $\psi$ for $\beta$, $\alpha$, and $\gamma$ is near zero.
\item Check bread conditioning and positive definiteness of the raw AIPW covariance.
\item Run the full derivative-validation pilot before a production simulation.
\item Inspect CFA step-size stability, directional-check error, and failed-replication log.
\item Compare predicted and empirical variance parameter by parameter; do not rely on a trace ratio alone.
\end{itemize}
